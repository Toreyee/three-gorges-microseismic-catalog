# Data license

The author-generated data products distributed in this repository were produced as part of the Three Gorges Reservoir microseismic catalog project and are made publicly available with the agreement of the project contributors for scientific research and reproducibility.

## Author-generated products

The distributed author-generated products include:

- the 6,344-event integrated manuscript catalog;
- the archived model-combination category products;
- selected REAL, VELEST, and hypoDD processing outputs;
- project-generated figure inputs and derived analysis products.

Unless otherwise stated, these author-generated data products are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).

Users may share and adapt these materials provided that appropriate credit is given to the authors and that the associated manuscript and repository are cited.

## Third-party material

Third-party software and externally sourced materials, where present, remain subject to their respective upstream licenses and terms. Their provenance and licensing information are documented in `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`, `NOTICE`, and the relevant repository subdirectories.

The complete continuous-waveform archive is not distributed through this repository because of its size and data-management requirements.
